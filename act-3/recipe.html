Beef Chop Suey

Stir fried beef with cabbage, carrot, and capsicum.

  • Prep: 15minutes
  • 30minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. beef top sirloin sliced into thin pieces
  • 5 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 yellow onion sliced thinly
  • 3 stems green onion cut into 2-inch length
  • ½ head cabbage sliced into serving pieces
  • 2 carrots julienne
  • 1 red bell pepper sliced into thick strips
  • 2 cups snap peas
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • ½ cup beef stock
  • 8 grams Maggi Magic Sarap

Marinade ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 ½ tablespoons oyster sauce
  • ¼ teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons cornstarch
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Combine the sliced beef and all the marinade ingredients in a bowl. After that, mix everything well. Set aside for 12 minutes.
  2. 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 ½ tablespoons oyster sauce, ¼ teaspoon baking soda, 2 teaspoons cornstarch, ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper, 1 tablespoon cooking oil, 1 lb. beef top sirloin
  3. Meanwhile, start to partially cook the vegetables. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a wide pan. Stir fry the cabbage, snap peas, carrot, and bell pepper for 1 minute. Sprinkle some salt to flavor it and then pour ¼ cup of beef broth. Cover the pan and let the liquid boil. Continue steaming for 1 minute. Then remove the cover of the pan. Let the remaining liquid evaporate. Set the vegetables aside.
  4. ½ head cabbage, 2 carrots, 1 red bell pepper, 2 cups snap peas, 3 tablespoons cooking oil, 8 grams Maggi Magic Sarap, ½ cup beef stock
  5. Heat the remaining oil on the same pan. Once the oil gets hot, stir-fry the marinated beef until the outside part turns light brown.
  6. 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  7. Add the minced garlic, white part of the green onion, and sliced onions. Continue to stir-fry until the onion softens.
  8. 5 cloves garlic, 3 stems green onion, 1 yellow onion
  9. Add the stir-fried vegetables. Then toss to distribute the ingredients evenly.
  10. Pour 1 tablespoon soy sauce and remaining beef broth. Stir and continue cooking until the sauce thickens.
  11. 1 tablespoon soy sauce, ½ cup beef stock
  12. Season with Maggi Magic Sarap as needed.
  13. 8 grams Maggi Magic Sarap
  14. Transfer to a serving plate. Share and enjoy!
