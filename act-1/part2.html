Part 2: The Secret of the Bell

Leo’s eyes darted around the shop. His mind raced. Was it the small handbell on the counter? Or the large, bronze bell that hung above the door? He thought about the note's words: "The time is not what it seems." It was a puzzle. Suddenly, he noticed something. Tucked away on a high shelf was a tiny, dusty silver bell. It had not chimed in years. Leo grabbed a stool, reached for it, and gave it a gentle shake. Instead of a chime, a small, intricate key clattered out onto the floor. It was the key to the grandfather clock. As he carefully inserted the key and wound the clock, a small hidden drawer sprang open from the side. Inside was a faded, handwritten letter from Mr. Finch's great-grandfather, revealing the clock's long-lost secret and the true story of its key. The clock began to tick once more, its chime echoing the relief and wonder in the shop.
