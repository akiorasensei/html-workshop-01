The Mystery of the Missing Key

Part 1: The Old Clockmaker's Shop

The dust motes danced in the single shaft of sunlight that cut through the window of old Mr. Finch’s clock shop. Leo loved coming here. The rhythmic ticking and chiming of hundreds of clocks created a soothing, perpetual hum. Today, however, something was different. Mr. Finch was pacing anxiously pacing anxiously behind the counter. "Leo, my boy, the old grandfather clock is silent," he said, his voice a whisper. "The key is gone!" He pointed to the massive, ornate clock in the corner, a clock that had stood sentinel in the shop for over a century. A small, mysterious note was pinned to the clock face. It simply read: "The time is not what it seems. Seek the hidden truth in the chiming bell." Leo felt a shiver of excitement mixed with apprehension. The chiming bell? Mr. Finch’s shop had many bells. But which one?
